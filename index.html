05 — 30-day plan

A month to test, not to buy.

Our suggested first month: we run Paths 1 and 2 side by side because they’re cheap and low-risk, and park 3–5 until one shows real demand. The aim is one repeatable, legally workable opportunity with a willing customer — not a cheap house in week one. The numbers are suggested starting bars, not rules.

Week 1

Groundwork

Let’s sit down and agree on time, capital, income goals, and the work you enjoy. We’ll book the one-hour lawyer consult and clear the conflict question with Rosa’s nonprofit employer. No property purchases this month.

Week 2

Build and reach out

We’ll build one sample report on a real Detroit listing and line up 8–10 investors from local investor groups. Alongside it, we’ll start the 10 short conversations with commercial owners and property managers.

Week 3

Show and listen

We’ll share the sample report and finish the owner conversations, noting what people already pay to solve and what they’d pay us. Suggested bars: 2 investors pay or commit, and 1 owner agrees to a small paid pilot.

Week 4

Decide together

We’ll look at the results side by side. If a path clears its bar, we plan a small paid pilot; if not, we adjust and try again. Paths 3–5 stay parked, and we won’t buy a property just to show activity.
